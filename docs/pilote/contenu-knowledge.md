## FAQ (10 entrées — une par carte)

| Question | Réponse |
|---|---|
| What happens if the weather is bad? | Safety first — if the boat can't go out we rebook you free of charge or refund your deposit in full. We confirm by 6am on the day. |
| Can children join? Is it safe for them? | Yes! Kids from [4] years old are welcome, life jackets in all sizes are provided and the crew keeps a close eye on little ones. |
| Do I need to know how to swim? | No — you can enjoy the boat and beaches without swimming, and we provide life jackets for everyone in the water. |
| Where and when is the hotel pickup? | We pick up from Chaweng, Lamai, Bophut and Maenam between 07:00 and 07:30 — we send your exact time the evening before. |
| How do I pay? | A ฿[500]/person deposit confirms your spot (transfer or card link); the rest in cash or card (+3%) on the day. |
| Can I cancel or change the date? | Yes, free of charge up to 24h before departure. Inside 24h the deposit is kept, but we always try to move you to another day first. |
| Is food included? What about allergies? | Lunch, water and fruit are included. Tell us about any allergy or vegetarian/halal preference when booking and we'll arrange it. |
| Is snorkeling gear included? | Yes — masks, snorkels and fins in all sizes are on board. You're welcome to bring your own too. |
| I get seasick — which trip should I pick? | The big boat to Angthong is the calmest ride. Take a motion-sickness pill 30 min before departure — we also keep some on board. |
| Can we book a private trip? | Absolutely — the private longtail charter (up to 6 people) is fully flexible. Tell us your date and group size and the team will confirm. |
